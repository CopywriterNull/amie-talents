# Edit rules

Read this before every edit. Every note Lenny gives on an edit becomes a new line here, so the same mistake never happens twice.

- No emojis in captions or on-screen text.
- Only one highlighted word on screen at a time.
- Captions stay out of the bottom 20% and the right rail; Instagram's UI covers both.
- Never cut mid-word or clip the first sound of a word. If a cut sounds clipped, widen the drop range to whole words.
- Restarts: keep the LAST complete attempt of a line and drop every earlier attempt, including the half-sentence lead-in.
- Drop fillers (um, uh, ah, like, you know) unless the line falls apart without them.
- Keep the CTA ("comment WORD...") intact and last. Nothing gets cut after it except dead air.
- The hook is the first words spoken. No dead air, throat-clearing or "so" before it.
