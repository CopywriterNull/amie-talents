Questions

What does it cost?

Your first email is free. After that it's $10 an email, in packs of 5, 20, 50 or 150 that never expire. Unlimited is $499 every 4 weeks for one brand and covers up to 250 emails a month. Agencies start at $999 a month.

Will Redraft ever send an email to my list?

No. It only creates draft templates in your Klaviyo account. You attach them to campaigns or flows and send them yourself.

What access does it need?

Read access to campaigns, flows, templates and images, plus write access to templates for pushing drafts. You can connect with Klaviyo sign-in or a private API key and revoke it any time.

Will it look like my brand?

It uses the fonts uploaded to your Klaviyo account, colors sampled from your emails and photos from your store. It keeps your brand and changes the structure.

Where do the claims and prices come from?

Only from your original send and your store. Every claim is checked against a source before you see the design, and anything unsourced is cut.

Do the GIFs work in Outlook?

Outlook desktop shows only the first frame, so the first frame always carries the full message. Every other major client plays the animation.

Is Redraft part of MailTail?

Yes. Redraft is MailTail's design product. MailTail's inbox-placement product gets your Klaviyo emails out of Gmail's Promotions tab and into Primary. Use either one, or both.

Can I edit the designs?

Yes. Ask for changes in plain English, swap modules between versions, or edit the template in Klaviyo after it's pushed.
