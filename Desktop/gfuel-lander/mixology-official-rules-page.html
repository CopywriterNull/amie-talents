JUDGING

There will be one (1) grand prize winner (“Grand Prize Winner”) and two (2) runner up prize winners (each, a “Runner-Up”) will be selected in this Contest. On or about August 7, 202, all Submissions will be initially reviewed by the Sponsor or a Sponsor representative for compliance with these Official Rules (including the Submission requirements). Thereafter, the eligible Submissions received will be judged and scored based on the following criteria: (40%) Creativity & Originality (How unique and inventive the recipe is); (25%) Product Integration (How effectively the featured G FUEL flavor is incorporated); (20%) Presentation (Visual appeal of the drink and/or content; (15%) Execution (Overall quality, clarity, and completeness of the Submission).

Each Submission will, for each of the above-described criteria, be scored with a raw score between 1-5 (with five (5) being the highest). For each Submission, after a (1-5) score has been determined for each criterion, each raw score will be “weighted”, considering the relative percentages shown above (in parentheses, next to each criterion). The “total score” for each scored Submission will be the total score of all of the above criteria (taking into account the above-described “weighting”). From among all Submissions received in the Contest Period, the one (1) Submission having the highest “total score” will be deemed the grand prize-winning Submission. The two (2) Submissions having the two (2) next highest “total scores” will be deemed the runner-up prize-winning Submissions. In the event of a tie, an additional judge will score each tied Submission (pursuant to the procedures described above) and the Submission with the highest total score pursuant to that subsequent scoring will be deemed the respective prize winner. In the event of a further tie, Sponsor (as applicable) shall, in their sole discretion, choose a prize winner (as applicable) based on its evaluation of which Submission is superior overall, taking the entire judging criteria into consideration.

The potential prize winners will be notified by the Sponsor via direct message to his/her Instagram or TikTok account within three (3) business days from the date in which the judging is complete. Each, potential prize winner will be required to supply his/her email address and shipping address for delivery of the respective prize. Sponsor will only make two (2) attempts to reach a potential prize winner via direct message within a seventy-two (72) hour time frame. In the event the Sponsor cannot reach a potential prize winner after two (2) attempts have been made, the original potential prize winner will be disqualified and an alternate prize winner (selected via the judging criteria outlined above) will be notified until the correct number of prize winners are confirmed.

All Prizes will be shipped within four (4) weeks or less from the date on which all prize winners are determined and verified via the process above. The first name, last initial, city, state and Submission of the prize winners may also be posted on Sponsor’s website and/or social media channels.
